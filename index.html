Frequently asked questions.

You can process up to 100 supported images in one batch, with a 100 MB per-file limit and a 500 MB total batch safety cap.

JPG, JPEG, PNG and WebP are supported for compress and convert. Other formats are shown in the converter and stay locked until their engine is added.

Upload 2 to 20 PDFs, drag them into order, then merge. The batch can be up to 250 MB and 500 pages. Password-protected PDFs are not supported.

Upload up to 10 PDFs at a time (100 MB each, 250 MB total, 100 pages per batch). Choose PNG or JPG, convert, then download images one by one or as a ZIP. Password-protected PDFs are not supported.

Upload up to 30 JPG, PNG or WebP images (100 MB each, 250 MB total). Reorder them, choose Fit to image, A4 or Letter pages, then create a single PDF. Large images may be safely resized when needed to complete the workflow.
